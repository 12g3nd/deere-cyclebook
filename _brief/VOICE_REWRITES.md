# Voice rewrites

Every sentence in CYCLEBOOK that should sound like you, not like a model. Write your version under each line, save the file, and tell me. I'll carry your wording into the redesigned pages.

**How to fill it in**

- Write it the way you'd say it to an interviewer across the table. Plain beats clever.
- Keep the numbers true. If you change a figure, flag it and I'll check it against Deere's releases.
- Leave `Yours:` blank to keep the line as-is (I'll still tighten punctuation). Write `cut` to delete it.
- Lines marked **tell** have a pattern that reads as machine-written. The note says which one.

Patterns to avoid: "not X, but Y" / "X instead of Y" contrasts, tidy groups of three, clever inversions ("outside the factory, inside the demand curve"), and words like *epistemology*, *lens*, *story*, *engine* used as flourish.

Page layouts are about to change, so some lines may move. The wording carries over wherever it lands.
