# Voice rewrites

These are the words on the seven cards as they render today (close $675.74, reported through Q3 FY2026). Each entry has four parts:

- the current line
- what reads as machine-written, if anything
- a proposed fix in your voice
- a blank `Yours:` line

Write your version, or `keep`, or `cut`, then tell me and I'll build it into the cards.

**How I wrote the proposals**

- **Stop-slop rules.** Every sentence gets a person or company doing the verb, and there are no adverbs. I cut "X, while Y" seesaws, tidy groups of three, em dashes, and anything that sounds like a pull quote.
- **Your voice, from your transmissions.** You write in first person and name the specific thing (CSB195, the GO train, Grant). You'd rather say a plain sentence than a clever one. "I'd rather fail than to never know" is a claim, and so is every finding here.
- **The card register is tighter than your blog.** A finding has to land for a finance exec in about three seconds, so the proposals keep your plainness and leave out the asides. Notes, buttons and the two lines at the end (pitch and resume bullet) have more room for you.
- **Live numbers.** Most findings recalculate when the data or the controls change. Rewrite the words and keep the slots, listed under each line. If your wording needs a word to flip (more/less, inside/above), say so and I'll wire it.
- **Length.** Line one of a finding fits about 95 characters. Line two fits about 110.
