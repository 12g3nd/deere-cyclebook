Why Deere?

In my last year of high school, my uncle bet me $15 I wouldn't wear an ugly camo John Deere shirt to school. I wore it every Tuesday. Eventually over forty people at school, a few teachers included, cleared out the local Walmart's stock of John Deere shirts. The company has held a special place for me ever since, so it was the obvious one to take apart.
