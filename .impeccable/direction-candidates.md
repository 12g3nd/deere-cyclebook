# Direction candidates (written before the roll)

**Mechanism.** CYCLEBOOK sets Deere's share price against its own reported earnings, management guidance and live farm-economy data, and labels every number actual, guidance or model so a reader can audit the thesis.

**Scene.** A hiring manager at a bank or research shop opens a published Power BI link on a laptop between meetings. Alone, ten minutes, no presenter. They read research notes and 10-Ks all day.

**Cultural home.** Sell-side equity research, and the North American farm-equipment economy.

**This surface must prove.** The author thinks like an analyst, and builds above the level of typical Power BI.

**Rut (excluded).**
- Category default: white KPI-card grid, blue/teal palette, donuts.
- Predictable opposite: dark Bloomberg-terminal pastiche, amber or neon on black.
- Incumbent world: cream paper, hairline rules, tracked mono micro-labels, side-stripe KPI cards. Itself an AI cluster.
- Literal brief reading: Deere green/yellow livery. One slot at most (#7).

## Ordered by resonance

1. **Initiation-of-coverage research note.** Front-page anatomy: rating and price box, key-data table, numbered "Exhibit N" charts each with a source line, thesis as "what we like / what worries us". *Family: sell-side publication.* The audience reads these daily, and source lines carry ACTUAL / GUIDANCE / MODEL natively.
2. **Engineering drawing sheet.** Title block with drawing number and revision table (model version, data as-of), dimension lines and leader callouts measuring the charts (peak-to-trough dimensioned), grid-reference border. *Family: engineering documentation.* Deere is an engineering company; dimensioning the cycle literally measures it; the revision block is provenance.
3. **Financial-press graphics desk (FT / WSJ).** Chart titles that state the finding, direct labels instead of legends, one highlight series, annotations on the line itself. *Family: journalism data graphics.* Hiring managers read these papers; finding-first titles prove analytical thinking.
4. **Precision-ag yield map (Operations Center).** Segment × quarter rendered as harvested swaths on a heat ramp, legend strip, field-grid basemap. *Family: geospatial agronomic graphics.* Deere's own data product; the cycle becomes a field.
5. **USDA WASDE balance sheet.** Dense monospaced tables, marketing-year columns, revision markers showing change since last report. *Family: government statistical publication.* The ag cycle's canonical document; revision markers carry guidance changes.
6. **Combine CommandCenter display.** Instrument panel, bar meters for margins, pages as display tabs, safety-yellow active states. *Family: vehicle instrument interface.* Model controls as a real instrument. Risk: dark UI drifts toward the terminal rut.
7. **Deere livery and operator's manual (literal reading).** Green/yellow blocks, numbered figure callouts, safety-sign triangles. *Family: industrial brand manual.*
